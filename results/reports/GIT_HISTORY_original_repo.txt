    Add real-robot handoff package with a porting guide
    
    The guide is the part that did not exist yet. It separates the system into three layers by
    porting difficulty and names the two things that actually break on hardware:
    
    - Success detection. The simulator's free, exact predicate feeds three places at once (BC pool
      admission, the maturity ledger, and the ACI risk update), so detector error contaminates
      learning, certification, and risk control simultaneously.
    - Paired replay. Episodes are deterministic from six elements in simulation, which is what
      makes H4b's paired bootstrap possible. Real hardware cannot reproduce an initial state, so
      the paper's "paired full-VLA replay under identical episode specifications" claim does not
      transfer and the test has to be redesigned.
    
    It also carries forward the failures worth not repeating — stale chunk tails at stage
    boundaries, controller state crossing episode boundaries, teacher outcomes leaking into the
    maturity ledger, band disjointness, and why long runs need a smoke gate with runtime
    assertions.
